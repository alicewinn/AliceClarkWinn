    OPERATIONAL ROADMAP

    The 90-Day Bestseller Timeline Preview

    A high-level preview of the exact operational milestone schedule documented inside the free 25-page launch checklist download.

    Phase 1: Foundation & Market Audit Weeks 12 to 8 Before Release

    Audit top 20 comp titles in your subgenre. Extract high-performing search phrases. Finalize professional typography-aligned cover art. Construct your high-tension reader magnet and configure automated email delivery sequences.

    Phase 2: Metadata & ARC Recruitment Weeks 8 to 4 Before Release

    Rewrite Amazon description using AIDA/PAS direct-response formulas. Assign 10 achievable micro-categories. Deploy 3-part ARC recruitment sequence across your email list and genre reviewer networks to lock in 50–150 advance readers.

    Phase 3: Pre-Order Ramp & Asset Assembly Weeks 4 to 1 Before Release

    Design Amazon A+ Content comparison modules. Pre-build tiered Amazon Sponsored Products and Meta Ad campaigns. Distribute digital ARC review copies with clear deadline reminders. Schedule promo site submissions.

    Phase 4: Launch Week Ad Blitz Release Week (Days 1 to 7)

    Deploy launch day email broadcast to author newsletter. Launch multi-tiered Amazon manual keyword and comp-product ad campaigns. Send day-3 review reminders to ARC team. Monitor category bestseller ranks and prune negative search terms daily.

    Phase 5: Post-Launch Sustainment Weeks 2 to 4 Post-Release

    Transition ad campaigns into evergreen auto/manual scaling structures. Review read-through rates to backlist titles. Deploy post-launch review chaser sequence to verified buyers. Lock in discount promos to prevent rank drop-offs.
