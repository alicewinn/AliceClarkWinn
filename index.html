THE HARD TRUTH

The Publishing Market Is More Crowded Than Ever.
Good Writing Alone Is No Longer Enough.

Over 4.5 million books enter the global marketplace each year. That is more than 12,000 new titles every single day across Amazon, Apple Books, Barnes and Noble, Kobo, and every other major retailer.

The heartbreaking reality of modern publishing is that extraordinary books disappear into obscurity every single day. Not because the story was weak. Not because the research was not thorough. Not because the author lacked talent. But because the book was launched into a vacuum with zero distribution strategy, no audience targeting, no ad infrastructure, and weak retail conversion.

Amazon's A10 ranking algorithm does not reward hard work or poetic prose on its own. It responds to conversion velocity, targeted click-through rates, category relevance, and sustained keyword traction over rolling 30-day and 90-day windows. If your book does not signal to the algorithm that readers are actively buying it, the algorithm will bury it on page 47 of the search results where no human being will ever find it.

Trying to guess your way through algorithmic advertising, metadata indexing, reader funnel architecture, and competitive positioning while simultaneously writing your next book is an exhausting, overwhelming uphill battle. You do not need more generic publishing advice from a blog post written in 2019. You need an experienced, data-first marketing partner who handles the entire execution and documents every single step in writing.

HOW IT WORKS

From Manuscript to Bestseller
In 6 Documented Steps

Every step of my process is structured, repeatable, and fully documented in writing. You will never be left guessing what happens next or wondering where your money is going. Here is exactly how we work together from start to finish.

01

Written Intake and Deep-Dive Analysis

Everything begins with your email. You submit your book details, genre, retailer links, past marketing efforts, and campaign goals through my structured Written Inquiry Form. Within 24 to 48 business hours, I personally review every piece of information you provide. I audit your current Amazon listing, your author platform, your social media presence, your email list (if you have one), and your competitive landscape. I identify exactly where the biggest revenue opportunities are hiding and where your current strategy is leaking money. You will receive a comprehensive written preliminary analysis directly in your inbox.

02

Custom Marketing Blueprint

Based on my analysis, I create a fully customized, 90-to-120-day marketing roadmap tailored specifically to your book, your genre, your budget, and your goals. This is not a cookie-cutter template I pull off a shelf and slap your name on. It is a detailed, day-by-day written action plan that covers everything from metadata optimization and blurb rewriting to ad campaign architecture, influencer outreach, email sequences, ARC coordination, and launch-day logistics. You will know exactly what is happening, when it is happening, why it is happening, and what results to expect at each stage.

03

Foundation Building and Asset Creation

Before we launch a single ad or send a single promotional email, we make sure every piece of your marketing infrastructure is bulletproof. This phase includes rewriting your book blurb for maximum conversion, optimizing your Amazon keywords and categories, designing Amazon A+ Content wireframes and copy, building your reader magnet funnel, setting up your email automation sequences, recruiting your ARC review team through structured outreach templates, and preparing all ad creatives and copy variations. Every asset is delivered to your inbox for your review and approval before anything goes live.

04

Campaign Execution and Launch Blitz

This is where the magic happens. I handle all the heavy lifting: building and launching your ad campaigns across Amazon, Meta, BookBub, and TikTok. Coordinating ARC distribution and review generation. Managing your email sequences. Executing your influencer outreach. Running your launch-day blitz. And monitoring everything in real time. You will receive detailed weekly performance reports via email with clear, jargon-free explanations of what is working, what is not, and what adjustments I am making.

05

Optimization and Scaling

Once the initial data rolls in, I shift into aggressive optimization mode. I analyze which ads are converting, which audiences are buying, which keywords are driving the cheapest clicks, which email subject lines are getting the highest open rates, and which landing pages are generating the most subscribers. Then I double down on the winners, ruthlessly cut the losers, test new variations, and scale your campaigns to maximize your return on every dollar spent. Every optimization decision is documented and explained in your weekly written report.

06

Post-Launch Sustainment and Long-Term Growth

Most marketers disappear after launch week. I do not. Once the initial buzz settles, I shift into sustainment mode. Optimizing your evergreen ad campaigns. Growing your newsletter funnel. Planning your next promotional windows. Setting up cross-promotions with other authors in your genre. Preparing for your next release. And building the systems that will support your author career for the next decade. Because real author empires are built over years, not weeks. All ongoing strategy and performance updates continue to be delivered via structured email reports.

FAQ

Got Questions?
I Have Got Detailed Answers.

How far in advance should I reach out before my book launches?

Ideally, I like to start working with authors 3 to 4 months before their release date. This gives us enough time to build a proper pre-launch strategy, grow your ARC team, set up your ad campaigns, and create all the marketing assets for maximum impact on day one. That said, I have also worked with authors who contacted me just 3 weeks before launch, and we still achieved fantastic results. The sooner you reach out via email, the more we can do, but it is never too late to start.

Do you work with self-published and traditionally published authors?

Both! Roughly 65% of my clients are indie and self-published authors using KDP, IngramSpark, or Draft2Digital, and about 35% are traditionally published or hybrid authors whose publishers are not doing enough to support their marketing. Regardless of your publishing path, the fundamentals of reader targeting, ad optimization, and conversion strategy remain the same.

How much does book marketing cost?

My packages range from one-time written audits starting at a few hundred dollars to comprehensive, multi-month launch campaigns and ongoing ad management retainers. During your free written proposal, I will assess your specific situation, goals, and budget, and recommend the most effective package for your needs. I believe in full transparency. You will always know exactly what you are paying for and what kind of return you can realistically expect. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Can you guarantee my book will become a number one bestseller?

No ethical marketer can guarantee a specific ranking because reader taste, market competition, and Amazon's algorithm are constantly changing. Anyone who promises you a guaranteed number one bestseller is being dishonest. What I can guarantee is that I will use every proven strategy, tool, and technique at my disposal to maximize your visibility, traffic, and sales potential. My track record speaks for itself: 75+ number one bestsellers and counting.

Why do you only communicate via email instead of calls?

Because written communication produces better results for authors. Every strategy, keyword list, ad report, and campaign plan is permanently documented in your inbox, searchable, and referenceable at any time. There are no forgotten verbal instructions, no time-zone scheduling headaches, and no wasted hours in meetings that could have been an email. My entire workflow is designed to maximize the time I spend actually working on your campaign rather than talking about it.

What genres do you specialize in?

Over the past decade, I have successfully marketed books in virtually every major genre, including Romance, Thriller and Suspense, Mystery, Science Fiction, Fantasy, Literary Fiction, Womens Fiction, Young Adult, Business and Entrepreneurship, Self-Help, Memoir, Health and Wellness, Christian and Inspirational, Childrens Books, True Crime, Cookbooks, History, Academic and Textbook, and many more. Three things. First, I have been doing this for over 10 years. I am not a newcomer who took a course last month. Second, I actually read your book before I market it, which means my campaigns feel authentic, not generic. Third, I treat your budget like it is my own money.
