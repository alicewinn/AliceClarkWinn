CASE STUDY 01 . FICTION

Debut Psychological Thriller: From Zero Platform to Amazon Top 10

Author: J. L.
Genre: Psychological Thriller and Suspense
Timeline: 90-Day Pre-Launch plus 30-Day Post-Launch
Starting Point: 18 email subscribers, mostly family

The Strategic Challenge

Launching a debut author with no existing brand, no reader platform, and no ad history against top-tier thriller authors on Amazon who have millions of readers and massive ad budgets. The psychological thriller category is one of the most saturated and competitive genres on the entire platform in 2026.

J. L. came to me four months before her debut novel's release date. She had a fantastic manuscript, a beautiful cover, and a compelling story, but absolutely no marketing infrastructure. Her email list had 18 subscribers (all family members). Her social media accounts were dormant. She had never run an ad in her life. And she was competing in one of the most saturated genres on Amazon against authors like Freida McFadden, Gillian Flynn, and Colleen Hoover.

The Written Strategy Implemented

Phase 1: Pre-Launch Foundation

Designed a reader magnet funnel featuring a 15-page prequel chapter. Created the landing page copy, the opt-in form, and the automated welcome sequence. Grew email subscribers from 18 to 1,850 in 60 days via targeted reader promos on BookFunnel and StoryOrigin.

Phase 2: Listing Optimization

Engineered a high-suspense blurb focusing on psychological tension and unreliable narrator tropes. Assigned 10 specific micro-categories with achievable bestseller ranking thresholds. Populated all 7 KDP backend keyword fields with high-intent search terms.

Phase 3: ARC Engine

Coordinated a 140-member advance reader team via structured email outreach templates. Managed the follow-up reminder sequences. Yielded 112 verified Amazon reviews on launch week.

Phase 4: Paid Advertising

Launched Amazon Sponsored Products campaigns targeting comp titles at controlled bids. Simultaneously launched Meta Ads targeting fans of those same authors with scroll-stopping hook copy.

CASE STUDY 02 . SERIES

Dead Fantasy Series: 420% Sales Increase in 60 Days

Author: M. R.
Genre: Epic Fantasy and Sword and Sorcery
Timeline: 60-Day Backlist Revitalization
Starting Point: 25 sales/month, 78% ACOS

The Strategic Challenge

M. R. had published 5 books in his epic fantasy series over the past 4 years. Book 1 had done reasonably well at launch, but sales across the entire catalog had flatlined to roughly 25 copies per month. His ad spend was high, his cost-per-click was unsustainable, and his read-through rate from Book 1 to Book 5 was a dismal 12%. He was considering abandoning the series entirely.

Low series read-through of 12% and excessive ad spend at $1.40 CPC with low conversion. Author was demoralized and on the verge of quitting the series altogether after four years of writing.

The Written Strategy Implemented

Listing Overhaul

Rewrote the blurbs for all 5 books to focus on clear subgenre tropes. Refreshed Book 1 cover typography and backend keywords.

Funnel Restructure

Updated back-matter in Books 1 to 4 with direct preview chapters and direct Amazon buy links for the immediate sequel in the series.

Pricing Promotion

Temporarily discounted Book 1 to $0.99 supported by targeted promos through Freebooksy, Fussy Librarian, and Robin Reads.

Ad Rebuild

Replaced broad, wasteful Amazon ad targets with exact-match comp author keywords and sponsored brand carousel ads highlighting the complete series box set.

The Documented Results

CASE STUDY 03 . NON-FICTION

Non-Fiction Breakthrough: From 50 Sales/Month to Six-Figure Annual Revenue

Author: Dr. A. P.
Genre: Health, Wellness, and Self-Help
Timeline: 6-Month Ongoing Campaign
Starting Point: 50 sales per month, no email list

The Strategic Challenge

Dr. A. P. had written an excellent, research-backed health book that had been published for 18 months but was stuck at roughly 50 sales per month. She had a modest social media following but no email list, no ad campaigns, and no Amazon optimization. She was about to give up on the book and focus solely on her clinical practice.

The Written Strategy Implemented

Amazon Listing Overhaul

Completely overhauled her Amazon listing with new subtitle optimized for search, new blurb focused on reader pain points and transformation, and new categories.

Multi-Platform Ad Launch

Launched Amazon Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns targeting high-intent health and wellness keywords.

Lead Magnet Funnel

Built a lead magnet (a free 7-day wellness challenge PDF) and grew her email list from 0 to 8,500+ subscribers in 6 months.

Podcast Booking Tour

Pitched and secured 3 podcast guest appearances on health and wellness shows with combined audiences of 200,000+ listeners.
