SERVICE FAQ

Common Questions About
My Services

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. I understand that book marketing is an investment. For larger packages, I offer flexible payment plans split across 2 to 4 installments. Mention it in your email inquiry and I will include payment plan options in your written proposal.

How quickly will I see results?

It depends on the service. Ad campaigns typically start generating data within the first 48 to 72 hours, and you should see meaningful sales trends within 2 to 4 weeks. Full launch campaigns build momentum over 2 to 3 months. Backlist revitalization usually shows significant improvement within 60 to 90 days.

Do I need a big budget for ads?

Not necessarily. I have run highly effective campaigns with ad budgets as low as $5 to $10 per day. The key is not how much you spend but how strategically you spend it. I will recommend a budget in your written proposal that aligns with your goals and comfort level.

Can you work with my existing team?

Absolutely. I frequently collaborate with publicists, cover designers, virtual assistants, and other marketing professionals via email. The more hands on deck, the better. I integrate seamlessly into your existing workflow.

What if I am not happy with the results?

Your satisfaction is my top priority. If at any point you feel the campaign is not meeting our agreed goals, we will have an honest written conversation, adjust the strategy, and course-correct immediately. I offer a satisfaction guarantee on select packages.
